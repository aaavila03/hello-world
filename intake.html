Security intake questionnaire

Thank you for completing this questionnaire. It helps us understand how your organisation protects the data and services you provide to us. It takes about 30–45 minutes.

  • Answer every question Yes, No or N/A. Use the comment box to add context, compensating controls or planned remediation dates – a “No” with a good explanation is better than an inaccurate “Yes”.
  • Your answers are saved automatically in this browser as you go, so you can come back later on the same device.
  • Have security documentation? Attach it below and you can skip questions it already covers – we'll complete those from your documents and contact you if anything is unclear.
  • When you're done, click Download response and email the file to your contact. Attached documents are included in the file. You can also print or save a PDF copy.
  • Received follow-up questions, or continuing on another device? Open the file you were sent or your earlier response here, answer, then download your response again.

Supporting documentation (optional)

Attach documents that describe your security program, for example a SOC 2 report, ISO 27001 certificate or statement of applicability, penetration test summary, security whitepaper, policies, or a questionnaire you've already completed for another customer.

PDF, Excel (.xlsx), TXT, Markdown or CSV · up to 5 files, 20 MB in total · save Word documents as PDF first.
